## Walkthrough

Cross Hyrule Field to Kakariko Village — a quiet settlement at the foot of Death Mountain, home to the Skulltula House (the cursed family that pays out rewards for tokens), a potion shop, and the Graveyard out back. Climb Death Mountain Trail dodging rolling boulders until you find a sleeping Goron blocking the entrance to Dodongo's Cavern; a bomb next to his ear wakes him and clears the way.

The dungeon is full of Baby Dodongos and fire traps; the **Bomb Bag** appears in a chest partway through, and from there you can blow open the cracked walls hiding Skulltulas and shortcuts. Follow the lava path to the circular room with the Dodongo statue — that's where the boss waits.

> 💡 The Deku Shield burns up in this dungeon's fire — consider buying the Hylian Shield at the Bazaar before you go in.

## Boss — King Dodongo

When he inhales to attack, throw a bomb straight into his mouth; he flips over stunned for a few seconds — rush in and slash repeatedly. Repeat the cycle three or four times.

Defeating him gets you the **Goron's Ruby Spiritual Stone** and frees the whole Goron tribe, starving after Dodongo cut off their rock supply.
