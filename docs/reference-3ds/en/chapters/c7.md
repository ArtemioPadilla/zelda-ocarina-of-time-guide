## Walkthrough

Climb up to Goron City as an adult and you'll find it nearly empty — Darunia is grieving what he believes was the death of his adopted son Link (the little Goron from your childhood survived, but the whole tribe fears the dragon Volvagia). Play **Saria's Song** to coax him back into dancing, and follow the path that opens up after the Hookshot to the summit, where Sheik teaches you the **Bolero of Fire**.

Buy the **Goron Tunic** before heading in if you haven't already — the heat of the Crater and the temple itself deals constant damage without it. Inside, the **Megaton Hammer** smashes the boulders blocking almost every new room; use it on the gold switch-plates too, to lower stone bridges over the lava.

> ⚠️ Without the Goron Tunic you lose hearts every few seconds in the Crater — head back to the Goron City shop if you're missing it.

## Boss — Volvagia

The dragon keeps surfacing from and diving back into the lava. The instant its head pops up, hit it with the Megaton Hammer before it can attack; if it's slow to surface, position yourself at the center of the stone ring and wait.

Defeating it wakes **Darunia as the Sage of Fire** and frees Goron City from its fear of the dragon.
