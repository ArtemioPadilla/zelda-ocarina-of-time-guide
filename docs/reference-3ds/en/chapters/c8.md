## Walkthrough

Lake Hylia has been drained, and the Lakeside Laboratory is under suspicion — the scientist inside lets you through once you show him you're carrying the Master Sword. The route to the temple runs through the **Ice Cavern** near Zora's Fountain (frozen over by King Zora himself, trapped in red ice): inside you get the **Iron Boots**, needed to sink and walk along the floor of the Water Temple.

Back at Lake Hylia, Sheik teaches you the **Serenade of Water**. The whole temple revolves around a central mechanism that raises and lowers the water level — switching levels with the Iron Boots on or off is the core mechanic of the entire dungeon. The **Longshot** comes from defeating Dark Link, an optional but highly recommended fight in a mirrored room.

> 💡 Mentally note which water level you need before you swim to a door — changing height while already underwater is much slower.

## Boss — Morpha

Hookshot (or Longshot) its floating nucleus to reel it in and slash it with your sword. If it catches you, it drags you inside its water body — move fast with the stick to break free before you lose too much health.

Defeating it wakes **Princess Ruto as the Sage of Water**.
