> **Order:** Epona’s Song as a child at Lon Lon Ranch; Epona as soon as you are an adult; Horseback Archery once the Gerudo stop chasing you.

## Epona’s Song (child)

Once you have Zelda’s Letter and have woken Talon at Hyrule Castle, go to Lon Lon Ranch, which in the remake is part of the Hyrule Field map, with no loading screen. Malon is in the middle of the corral with the foal Epona. Talk to her a few times and take out the Ocarina (D-pad ←): she teaches you **Epona’s Song** (X Y A X Y A). From then on Epona knows you.

## Getting Epona (adult)

1. Go back to the ranch during the day. Ingo runs it now and lets you ride in the corral if you pay (10 Rupees in the original game).
2. Play Epona’s Song in the corral: Epona comes to you. Mount her and talk to Ingo on horseback.
3. He challenges you to a one-lap race (in the original game he bet 50 Rupees). Get ahead early and block him when he tries to overtake, but always keep one boost in reserve to recover stamina.
4. If you win, Ingo asks for a rematch and bets Epona herself. Beat him again.
5. Ingo shuts the gates. Gallop straight at the outer fence at speed and Epona jumps it.

From then on you call Epona by playing her song in Hyrule Field and other open areas. With her you jump the broken bridge in Gerudo Valley and make the Big Poes appear. In the original game enemies can’t hurt you on horseback and the Bow is the only item you can use (not yet confirmed for the remake).

## Horseback Archery

It is at the back of Gerudo’s Fortress. You need the Gerudo membership card, which you get for freeing the four carpenters, and you play during the day.

- Each attempt cost 20 Rupees in the original game (not yet confirmed for the remake).
- Epona runs the course on her own while you fire up to 20 arrows at the targets. The closer to the centre, the more points.
- **1,000 points or more:** a **Piece of Heart**. Remake guides confirm it.
- **1,500 points or more**, on a later attempt: a **bigger quiver**. That is the original game’s figure; it is not yet confirmed for the remake.

Aim with ZR; you can use the buttons or motion controls, and the third-person view lets you track the target while Epona runs. One remake guide places this after the Spirit Temple; another only requires the Gerudo to no longer be hostile.

## Afterwards

Once Epona is free, Ingo calms down and the ranch goes back to Malon. Her obstacle course is waiting for you there (see the Lon Lon Ranch step).
