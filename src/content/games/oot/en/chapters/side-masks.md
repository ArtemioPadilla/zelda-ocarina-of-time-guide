> **Order:** as a child, as soon as you show Zelda’s Letter to the guard at the north gate of Kakariko Village. The last sale may have to wait until you have all three Spiritual Stones.

The Happy Mask Shop opens in Hyrule Castle Town, in the north-east of the square, next to the street up to the Temple of Time. It doesn’t sell: it lends you a mask, you sell it to someone and then pay the shop what it costs. Once you pay, it lends you the next one. In the remake masks go on with D-pad ↑ (headgear) and, for the first time, they also work as an adult.

## The four sales

1. **Keaton Mask.** Put it on and talk to the guard at Kakariko’s north gate, the one who told you about the shop: he wants it for his son.
2. **Skull Mask.** Take it to the Skull Kid in the Lost Woods: enter from Kokiri Forest and take the first path on the left. He pays less than it’s worth, so the difference comes out of your own pocket.
3. **Spooky Mask.** Sell it to the boy who walks around the Kakariko Graveyard during the day, Dampé’s young fan. If it’s night, play the Sun’s Song (A R X A R X).
4. **Bunny Hood.** Sell it to the Running Man, who jogs around the northern half of Hyrule Field. In the original game he only shows up once you have all three Spiritual Stones (not yet confirmed for the remake). According to Nintendo Life, in the remake he pays you with a Gold Rupee worth 200.

> 💡 Before you sell the Hood, spend some Rupees or get the Adult’s Wallet: the remake’s starting wallet holds 200 and anything over that is lost.

In the original game the masks cost 10, 20, 30 and 50 Rupees. The remake makes shops pricier, so bring some margin (prices not yet confirmed for the remake).

## The Mask of Truth

When you pay for the Hood, the shopkeeper lends you the **Mask of Truth**, a Sheikah relic, and opens the other half of the shop: the Goron, Zora and Gerudo masks, which are just for show. From then on you can borrow any mask you have already sold.

With it on:

- Gossip Stones tell you secrets and hints.
- New in the remake: you can read some characters’ thoughts. The dialogue box turns purple, as with the Lenses of Truth.

## Mask extras

- At the Deku theatre in the Lost Woods, the Skull Mask gets you a bigger Deku Stick capacity and the Mask of Truth more Deku Nuts (original-game facts; not yet confirmed for the remake).
- The Bunny Hood stops Stalchildren from appearing at night in Hyrule Field, and the Skull Mask stops Keese and crows from attacking you (original-game facts).
- The Young Link and Young Zelda amiibo unlock retro masks based on the Nintendo 64 models.

In the adult era the shop is in ruins: if you still have a sale to make, turn back into a child at the Temple of Time.
