With Ganondorf beaten, Zelda is freed from the crystal, but the castle starts to come down. This is the game’s last stretch: an escape with Zelda and the final battle against Ganon.

> **Order:** free Zelda → go down the tower with her → castle exit → Ganon in the ruins → the sealing → ending.

## The escape

The escape from Ganondorf’s Castle is one of the most changed parts of the remake:

- **There is no timer anymore.** Take your time going down, pick up hearts and do not take risks on the jumps.
- **Zelda leads and helps you.** At one point she holds back a horde of enemies with her powers while you move on, and near the exit she freezes the ReDeads blocking the way.
- **Mind the Hookshot.** Outside the castle there is a gap you can only cross with the Hookshot (or the Longshot). If it is not equipped, open the bar with L or R.

Stay close to Zelda: when an enemy or a fire barrier blocks her path, defeat the enemies in that room so she can carry on. Use the pots and grass to top up hearts and magic before you leave: you will need arrows and magic for the finale.

## Boss: Ganon

As you leave, Ganondorf rises from the rubble and transforms into **Ganon**. A ring of fire closes off the arena and, as the fight starts, Ganon knocks the Master Sword out of your hand: it ends up stuck on the far side of the fire.

**Before the fight:** bring the **Megaton Hammer** or, better, the Biggoron Sword, since those are the weapons you have left without the Master Sword. Make sure you have magic for several Light Arrows, green potions or fairies.

**Phase 1, without the Master Sword:**

1. Ganon is only vulnerable on his **tail**. Attack him head-on and he sweeps you away with his blades.
2. Shoot a **Light Arrow** (ZR) at his face to stun him for a moment.
3. While he is stunned, dash (hold A) between his legs or around him and hit his tail with the Hammer or the Biggoron Sword.
4. Repeat. When he charges at you, step aside; his ground slams throw up debris.

After several hits to the tail, Ganon collapses and the ring of fire opens a way to the Master Sword. Zelda calls out to you: go and get it.

**Phase 2, with the Master Sword:** the pattern is the same, but every Master Sword blow to the tail now does much more damage. When Ganon falls for the last time, Zelda and the Sages hold him down: walk up and press Y to deliver the final blow with the Master Sword.

Reviews of the remake mention tweaked patterns and the odd rare new attack for bosses, without saying which (not yet confirmed for Ganon). If an attack that is not described here catches you off guard, back off, watch and return to the Light Arrow and tail cycle.

## The ending

The Sages seal Ganon in the Sacred Realm. Zelda plays the Ocarina of Time to send Link back to his own time, and the ending travels across Hyrule with the characters you met. Enjoy the voiced cutscenes: the remake has full voice acting in several languages.

## After the ending

- **There is no New Game+, Hero Mode or Master Quest** at launch. Dataminers have found Master Quest data that would arrive with downloadable content, but Nintendo has announced nothing.
- **The Hall of Fallen Foes** lets you rematch Ganondorf, Ganon and every boss against the clock. Clear one of the boss gauntlets and Kaepora Gaebora delivers a trophy to Link’s house in Kokiri Forest.
- If you want to keep completing things, load your manual save from before crossing the castle bridge. What the game saves after the credits is not documented yet (not yet confirmed for the remake).

> **Don’t leave without:** a manual save from before the ending and, for the fight, the Megaton Hammer or the Biggoron Sword, magic for the Light Arrows and bottles of fairies.
