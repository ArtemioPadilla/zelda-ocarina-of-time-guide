> **Order:** as an adult, once you have Epona, the Fairy Bow (Forest Temple) and at least one empty bottle.

There are 10 Big Poes spread across Hyrule Field. Each soul you bottle sells at the Ghost Shop in Hyrule Castle Town. In the remake the prize comes sooner: the Poe Collector gives you a bottle for 5 Big Poes, half as many as in the original game, and all 10 also earn you a Gold Rupee worth 200.

## Finding them

- In the remake every spawn point has a clearly visible **bright purple lantern**. The Big Poe statue in the Hall of Fallen Foes sums it up: a suspicious-looking lantern gives them away.
- Call Epona with Epona’s Song (X Y A X Y A) and ride near the lantern. One remake guide suggests jumping the purple-lit fences and logs to make it appear. On foot you usually get a regular Poe instead.
- The remake’s Hyrule Field has new rivers, ruins and groves, so don’t trust Big Poe maps from the original game.

## Catching them

1. As soon as it appears, it flees. Chase it on horseback and aim the Bow with ZR; you can aim with the buttons or with motion controls, and the view is third-person.
2. Hit it with two arrows (according to the same guide) before it vanishes.
3. When it falls it leaves its soul floating. Equip an empty bottle and catch it with ZR before it disappears.

You can carry as many Big Poes as you have empty bottles. The remake has 5 bottles: keep one for a fairy and hunt in batches.

## Selling them

The Ghost Shop is in the old guardhouse at the entrance to Hyrule Castle Town, in the adult era. Each Big Poe adds 100 points to your card:

- **500 points (5 Big Poes):** an empty bottle. It is the only adult bottle.
- **1,000 points (10 Big Poes):** a Gold Rupee worth 200.

How many Rupees each soul pays is not confirmed: it was 50 in the original game and one remake guide says 30. Leave room in your wallet.

> 💡 Sell each batch as soon as your bottles are full: points build up on your card, so you don’t need to bring all 10 at once.

## Why it’s worth it

The bottle is the big prize: with the remake’s five bottles you can take several fairies to the final bosses, or keep Blue Fire and bugs for the soft-soil Skulltulas. The Gold Rupee helps with the Great Fairy offerings, which go up to 500 Rupees.
