When you return to Kakariko Village as an adult you find it in flames: something invisible bursts out of the well and tears through the village. Sheik holds it back and tells you Impa went to seal it in the Shadow Temple. To get in there you need to see the unseen, and that means being a child again for a while.

> **Order:** Kakariko in flames → Nocturne of Shadow → as a child: windmill and Song of Storms → Bottom of the Well → Dead Hand → Lenses of Truth → as an adult: graveyard → Din’s Fire on the torches → Shadow Temple → Hover Boots → Bongo Bongo → Shadow Medallion.

## Nocturne of Shadow

After the scene, Sheik teaches you the **Nocturne of Shadow** (Y A A L Y A R), which warps you to the top of the graveyard, in front of the temple entrance. Do not go in yet: without the Lenses you cannot get far.

## Bottom of the Well (as a child)

Turn back into a child at the Temple of Time and go to the Kakariko windmill. Play the **Song of Storms** (L R X L R X) inside: the windmill spins faster and the village well drains. Crawl down.

It is a maze-like mini-dungeon full of fake walls, invisible floors and pits. You do not have the Lenses yet, so hug the walls, follow Navi’s hints (→) and test suspicious walls. Positions are from the base game (not yet confirmed for the remake).

- **Central room:** play Zelda’s Lullaby at the Triforce mark to drain the water from the basement.
- **Small keys:** they open the cells around the central room. Behind them are **Gold Skulltulas #75, #76 and #77**: all three need a small key and the Boomerang to fetch the token.
- **Dead Hand:** in the basement. Its hands come up through the floor and grab you. In the remake, **if you defeat every hand, Dead Hand surfaces** (the hands come back afterwards). When it shows itself, hit its head with the sword, back off and repeat.
- **Lenses of Truth:** Dead Hand’s prize. In the remake they are goggles on D-pad **↑** (headgear) and drain magic while you wear them; the effect is a purple eye opening. Take them off as soon as you no longer need them.

With the Lenses you can now win the Castle Town Treasure Chest game, at night and as a child: **Piece of Heart #4**. And since you know the Song of Storms, play it for the Zora’s River frogs for **Piece of Heart #14**.

## The temple entrance

As an adult, use the Nocturne to reach the top of the graveyard. The ring of torches lights all at once with **Din’s Fire**. According to Zelda Wiki, in the remake the Death Mountain Great Fairy gives it, not the castle one; other guides keep the original split (not yet confirmed). If you do not have it, go and get it before you climb up.

## Shadow Temple

The darkest temple in the game: invisible floors and walls, unseen scythes, guillotines and a river you cross by boat. Wear the Lenses in the key rooms and refill magic with jars and fairies. The layout is the base game’s (not yet confirmed for the remake); no changes to this dungeon have been documented.

- **Entrance:** with the Lenses, find the fake wall and the real path. Fire the Hookshot at targets that only appear through the Lenses.
- **Spinning statue:** turn the skull statue until it points at the real skull (the one you see through the Lenses) to open the way. The **Map** and **Compass** are in this first part (room not yet confirmed).
- **Hover Boots:** another Dead Hand guards them; beat it as in the well. They sit on D-pad **↓** alongside the Iron Boots and let you walk on thin air for a moment. They slide: let go of the stick before you reach the edge.
- **Trap section:** invisible scythes, guillotines, spikes dropping from the ceiling and giant skull jars. Take it slowly, Lenses on, and jump (X) only when you can see floor.
- **Wind room:** the fans push you into the void; put on the Iron Boots and walk against the wind.
- **The river:** play Zelda’s Lullaby at the mark on the dock to set the boat off. Stalfos attack during the ride. At the end, shoot the Bomb Flowers on the pillar with the Bow so it falls into a bridge.
- **Boss Key and door:** past the maze of invisible walls at the end (Lenses on throughout). Cross the last stretch in the Hover Boots to the boss door.

**Gold Skulltulas #78–82:** #78 in the cell of the room with the rising and falling spikes, #79 behind the big skull jar, #80 beyond the room of invisible scythes, #81 in the room where the boat docks (with the Scarecrow’s Song) and #82 in the room with the three spinning skull jars.

## Boss: Bongo Bongo

You drop onto a giant drum that Bongo Bongo beats with two hands. Put on the Lenses (↑): without them you cannot see his body.

1. Target each hand with ZL and shoot it with an arrow (or the Hookshot) to stun it. Do both quickly.
2. With the hands stunned, Bongo Bongo opens his big eye: shoot it with an arrow.
3. He drops onto the drum: rush in and land as many slashes as you can. Repeat.

The drumbeats bounce you around; the Hover Boots reduce the bounce. Do not stand by the edge when a hand clenches into a fist. Once he falls, press A to pick up **Heart Container #7**. Impa awakens as the Sage of Shadow and gives you the **Shadow Medallion**.

## After the temple

In Kakariko, Longshot up to a rooftop and talk to the man up there for **Piece of Heart #26**. For the next temple you still need the Silver Gauntlets, which you get as a child in the desert.

> **Don’t leave without:** the Lenses of Truth, the Hover Boots, Gold Skulltulas #75–77 in the well (key and Boomerang, as a child) and Piece of Heart #4 from the Treasure Chest game.
