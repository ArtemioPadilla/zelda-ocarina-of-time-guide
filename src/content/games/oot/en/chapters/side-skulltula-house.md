> **Order:** from your first visit to Kakariko Village, as a child. Come back every time you reach another 10 tokens: the sooner you have the Echo Gem and the wallets, the better.

In Kakariko Village there is a grey house full of cobwebs (in the remake the villagers call it “the house covered in cobwebs”). Inside lives a rich family that its greed turned into Skulltulas. Every time you destroy a Gold Skulltula you keep its token; for every 10 tokens, one of the sons gets his human form back and gives you a prize.

## Rewards in the remake

- **10 tokens: Adult’s Wallet.** Holds **500 Rupees** (the remake’s starting wallet holds 200).
- **20 tokens: Echo Gem.** It replaces the Stone of Agony from earlier versions. Equip it and it flashes and vibrates when a Gold Skulltula is nearby, even at spots where one only appears at night.
- **30 tokens: Giant’s Wallet.** Holds **1,000 Rupees**.
- **40 tokens: 20 Bombchus.** Zelda Wiki and two remake guides say 20; one guide that copies Nintendo 64 data says 10.
- **50 tokens: Piece of Heart.**
- **100 tokens: Gold Rupee worth 200.** The father gets his form back and gives you one every time you leave the house, come back in and talk to him.

> ⚠️ If you have an old guide, don’t trust its prizes: the remake has no Stone of Agony and the wallets hold twice as much.

## The 100 Gold Skulltulas

- The total is still **100**: **56** in the overworld and **44** inside dungeons.
- The per-area counts match the previous version except two: **Hyrule Field has 3** (was 2) and **Lon Lon Ranch 3** (was 4).
- According to the remake guides, “most” are where they were, but some moved and nobody has published which ones yet.
- **Ten** are under soft soil: release a bug from a bottle on the patch and the Skulltula comes out.
- Many only appear at night. Play the Sun’s Song (A R X A R X) or sleep in a bed to change the time.

## Tips

1. Get the Echo Gem early: at 20 tokens it saves you a lot of searching.
2. Always carry a bottle of bugs for the soft-soil Skulltulas.
3. Many are far away or behind bars: knock them down and collect the token with the Boomerang as a child or the Hookshot as an adult.
4. The remake’s map shows each area’s collectible status: use it to see where you are missing some.

The bigger wallets also come in handy for the Great Fairy offerings (up to 500 Rupees) and for buying the Business Scrubs’ Pieces of Heart (100 Rupees each).
