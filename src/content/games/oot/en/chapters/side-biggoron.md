> **Order:** as an adult. You can start as soon as you leave the Temple of Time, but you can’t finish until you thaw King Zora with Blue Fire from the Ice Cavern. With Epona, the Longshot and the Water Temple done, the timed legs are much easier.

The Biggoron Sword is the strongest sword in the game: it deals double the Master Sword’s damage, but it takes both hands and you can’t carry a shield while it is equipped. In the remake the chain keeps the same order, although several items have new English names and the timers are different. The Threads of Time quest log reminds you which step you are on.

## Before you start

- An empty bottle for the Blue Fire.
- Epona to jump the broken bridge in Gerudo Valley, or the Longshot.
- The Sun’s Song (A R X A R X) to switch between day and night.
- Don’t use warp songs during the timed legs: the item spoils.

## The eleven trades

1. **Pocket Egg.** As an adult, talk to the Cucco Lady in Kakariko Village. The egg hatches at the next dawn; play the Sun’s Song twice if you don’t want to wait.
2. **Pocket Cucco.** Talon is asleep on the ground floor of the big house in the middle of Kakariko, next to the steps by the well. Use the Cucco on him to wake him (he goes back to Lon Lon Ranch), then return to the Cucco Lady: she swaps the Cucco for Cojiro.
3. **Cojiro.** This blue Cucco never spoils. Take it to the Lost Woods and look for the man asleep under a tree in one of the first clearings: Nintendo Life puts him straight ahead of the Kokiri Forest entrance, another guide on the left. He is the Master Craftsman’s son and gives you the Odd Mushroom.
4. **Odd Mushroom: 4:00.** Take it to the old woman in Kakariko’s back potion shop. Shortcut: go into the shop with the purple door, at the north end of the village, and out through its back door. You get the Odd Poultice.
5. **Odd Poultice.** Go back to the same Lost Woods clearing. Fado is there now and gives you the Poacher’s Saw.
6. **Poacher’s Saw.** Hand it to the Master Craftsman, in the tent past the broken bridge in Gerudo Valley. He gives you the Broken Goron Sword.
7. **Broken Goron Sword.** Take it up to Biggoron, at the summit of Death Mountain. The quickest way is the Bolero of Fire to the Crater, then the south exit. He gives you the Prescription.
8. **Prescription.** Show it to King Zora in Zora Domain and he gives you the Eyeball Frog.
9. **Eyeball Frog: 2:30.** Run it to the scientist at the Lake Hylia Lakeside Laboratory. After the Water Temple, Zora Domain thaws and you can dive through the tunnel down to the lake. He makes the Speciality Eye Drops.
10. **Speciality Eye Drops: 3:30.** Take them to Biggoron: ride Epona across Hyrule Field, through Kakariko and up the mountain trail, or use the shortcut from Goron City to the Crater. He gives you the Biggoron Sword Receipt.
11. **Biggoron Sword Receipt.** Biggoron takes three in-game days to forge the sword. Play the Sun’s Song six times and come back: the Biggoron Sword is yours.

> ⚠️ The three timers (4:00, 2:30 and 3:30) come from the first remake guides, which agree with each other; a third guide gives 4:00 for the Eye Drops. Plan for the shorter one.

## Medigoron

One remake guide says Medigoron sells a sword in Goron City for 200 Rupees (not yet confirmed for the remake). You don’t need it for the chain.

## Decisions

The Biggoron Sword takes away your shield. Use it against bosses and slow enemies; switch back to the Master Sword and the Mirror Shield when you need to block or reflect.

Some item names changed in the remake (Odd Poultice, Broken Goron Sword, Speciality Eye Drops, Biggoron Sword Receipt); old guides use Odd Potion, Broken Goron’s Sword, Eyedrops and Claim Check.
