The last Spiritual Stone is with the Zoras. Follow Zora’s River upstream from the east side of Hyrule Field. At the end of the road is Zora Domain, where the king has lost his daughter and the guardian god, Lord Jabu-Jabu, is ill.

> **Order:** Zora’s River → Zelda’s Lullaby at the waterfall → Zora Domain → Silver Scale → Ruto’s Letter in Lake Hylia → the king moves aside → Zora Fountain → a fish for Jabu-Jabu → Lord Jabu-Jabu’s Belly → Barinade → Zora Sapphire.

## Zora’s River

The remake’s river has a new look, with spruces and ruins, but the route is the same. Early on you meet the Magic Bean salesman: buy several for the soft soil you find (on Death Mountain Trail, in the Lost Woods, in the graveyard and elsewhere). Shops charge more in the remake, so watch your wallet.

- **Piece of Heart #12:** on top of a pillar. The original’s Cucco jump reportedly no longer works; Nintendo Life suggests combining bombs and a Cucco (not yet confirmed).
- **Piece of Heart #13:** on a platform by the waterfall. Glide there with a Cucco.
- **Pieces of Heart #14 and #15:** the river’s singing frogs. The first needs the Song of Storms (learned as an adult) and the second needs every song. Leave them for the end.
- **Gold Skulltulas #39 and #40:** in the tree by the entrance (roll into the trunk) and on the ladder at the foot of the waterfall, at night. **#41 and #42** are adult ones, with the Hookshot. Game8 mentions a new bug hole near the river (not yet confirmed).

At the end of the river, step onto the platform with the Triforce symbol and play **Zelda’s Lullaby** (Y X A Y X A): the waterfall parts.

## Zora Domain and Ruto’s Letter

King Zora tells you Ruto has gone missing. Before looking for her, get the **Silver Scale**: talk to the Zora at the top of the domain and win his diving game by collecting the Rupees he throws into the water. With the scale you dive deeper.

Dive through the tunnel at the bottom of the domain: you come out in **Lake Hylia**, which in the remake is part of the Hyrule Field map. In the water, near the shore, is a bottle holding **Ruto’s Letter**. Take it to the king: he reads it, shuffles aside and gives you back the empty bottle. Fill that bottle with a fish before moving on (there are some in the domain itself and in the Zora shop).

- **Piece of Heart #16:** light every torch in the domain with a Deku Stick to open the chest.
- **Piece of Heart #17:** catch a big fish at the Lake Hylia fishing pond (in the remake it takes about 55 cm and fishing costs 30 Rupees, according to the first guides).
- **Piece of Heart #18:** at the bottom of the lake laboratory’s tank; you need the Golden Scale, won by fishing as an adult.
- **Lake Gold Skulltulas:** **#47** (soft soil in front of the laboratory, with bugs), **#48** (on the island monument, at night) and **#49** (on the back wall of the laboratory, with the Boomerang). **#50 and #51** are adult ones.

## Zora Fountain

Go behind the throne. The remake’s Zora Fountain has a new layout, but Jabu-Jabu still floats in the water. Release the fish from the bottle in front of him (select it with ZR): Jabu-Jabu opens his mouth and swallows you. **Gold Skulltula #44** is in the tree on the south-east shore and **#45** is on a wall you can see from the floating log, with the Boomerang. **#46** is an adult one.

## Lord Jabu-Jabu’s Belly

An organic dungeon with electric Biri and Bari, Shabom bubbles and holes between floors. In the remake the tunnels are **colour-coded**, which makes finding your way easier. In the original there were no small keys and no Boss Key here (not yet confirmed for the remake).

- **Entrance:** shoot the uvula hanging from the ceiling with the Slingshot to open the door.
- **Ruto:** you find her early in the big central room. She drops down a hole: follow her. From then on you have to carry her (A to lift her). If you set her down somewhere, she waits there. Use her as a weight on floor switches and carry her onto the rising platforms.
- **Boomerang:** in a chest in the Stingers’ room, shortly after you team up with Ruto. It is your key tool: it stuns Biri and Bari, fetches distant items and cuts the tentacles.
- **The tentacles:** each coloured tunnel ends in a tentacle blocking the way. Target it with ZL, throw the Boomerang with ZR and open the next stretch. The **Map** and **Compass** are along those stretches (exact chests not yet confirmed).
- **Big Octo:** in the flooded room, a giant octopus circles the arena. Throw the Boomerang to make it turn around and slash the soft spot on its back. In the remake this arena was redesigned and no longer has the spinning spikes (not yet confirmed).
- **On to the boss:** after Big Octo, Ruto gets back what she was looking for and is carried out of the room. Climb up, hit the switch with the Boomerang and enter the boss room.

**Gold Skulltulas #52–55:** #52 on the corridor wall just before the boss room, #53 and #54 behind the west and east holes of the central room (fetch them with the Boomerang) and #55 on the vines of the east room on the lower floor.

## Boss: Barinade

Barinade is attached to the ceiling by three tentacles and surrounded by Bari jellyfish.

1. Target each tentacle with ZL and cut it with the Boomerang (ZR). Keep moving: the bolts on the floor chase you.
2. Once it breaks free, it spins surrounded by Bari. Throw the Boomerang at its body to stun it and scatter the jellyfish, and slash them (Y).
3. Without its Bari shield, stun its body with the Boomerang and hit it with the sword while it is still. Repeat.

Deku Nuts also stun the Bari. Once it falls, press A to pick up **Heart Container #3**.

## After the dungeon

Ruto gives you the **Zora Sapphire** in her own way: as an engagement token. With all three Spiritual Stones, head back to Castle Town. Before you go, the Zora Fountain Great Fairy (behind a bombable rock on the islet) gives you Farore’s Wind if you already have the Magic Meter. In the remake it costs 2 magic and sends you back to the exact spot you marked.

> **Don’t leave without:** the Silver Scale, the Ruto’s Letter bottle (now empty) and the Gold Skulltulas you can now collect with the Boomerang: #4 in the Great Deku Tree, #13 and #14 at the ranch, #25 in the graveyard and #45 at the fountain.
