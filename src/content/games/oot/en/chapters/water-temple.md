Lake Hylia has dried up and Zora Domain is frozen. In the remake even the passage from the lake to the Zoras is covered in ice. Climb Zora’s River as an adult: King Zora is trapped in red ice and the only lead takes you to Zora Fountain, where the Ice Cavern opens. The Water Temple keeps its three-water-level mechanic, but the remake makes it far easier to live with.

> **Order:** frozen Zora Domain → Zora Fountain → Ice Cavern → Iron Boots → Serenade of Water → Blue Fire → Zora Tunic → Lake Hylia → Water Temple → Dark Link → Longshot → Morpha → Water Medallion.

## Zora Fountain and the Ice Cavern

Cross the icy domain and go behind the throne. The fountain is full of ice floes: jump from one to the next (X) for **Piece of Heart #27**. The Ice Cavern entrance is at the back; in the remake it is visibly caved in during the child era.

Inside are Freezards, Ice Keese and White Wolfos. There is no Stone of Agony and no Piece of Heart here anymore: the piece that used to be in this cavern is now sold by a Business Scrub in Death Mountain Crater (chapter 7). The original’s Silver Rupees are now **silver gems** that fly into the door lock by themselves when you collect them.

- **Spinning blade room:** collect the silver gems while dodging the blades. **Gold Skulltula #67** is here, with the Hookshot.
- **Blue Fire:** fill a bottle at the blue flames and use it to melt the red ice blocking doors and chests. Always carry a free bottle.
- **Ice block room:** push the blocks to clear a path across the slippery floor. **Gold Skulltula #68** is there; **#69** is in the Compass room.
- **Iron Boots:** in the chest at the back, after beating a White Wolfos. In the remake they sit on D-pad **↓** (footwear) and go on and off instantly, with no menu. It is the change that does the most to ease the Water Temple.

As you leave with the boots, Sheik teaches you the **Serenade of Water** (L R A A Y), which warps you to Lake Hylia.

Go back to the domain and melt King Zora’s red ice with Blue Fire: he gives you the **Zora Tunic**, which lets you breathe underwater. With the boots on, sink to the bottom of the fountain for **Piece of Heart #28**. **Gold Skulltula #43** is by the domain’s frozen waterfall, with the Hookshot.

## Lake Hylia and the entrance

Put on the Zora Tunic, go down into the lake and walk along the bottom in the Iron Boots to the temple entrance under the islet. Hookshot the target above the gate to open it. While you are here, **Gold Skulltula #50** is in a crate at the bottom of the laboratory’s flooded room (with the boots).

## Water Temple

The key is the water level. There are **three heights**, changed by playing Zelda’s Lullaby at the Triforce marks. In the remake, **coloured lights** show the level, some walls are now **lattices** you can see the next room through, and several rooms were adjusted. Cubed3 even says the infamous key everyone missed is now a Rupee in a chest (not yet confirmed). Locations in this section are from the base game (not yet confirmed for the remake).

- **Ruto:** she greets you in the central room and swims up. Follow her to the Triforce mark on the upper floor and play the Lullaby: the water drops to the **lowest level**.
- **Low level:** explore the lower floors. You will find the **Map**, the **Compass** and several small keys. Use a key to enter the central tower: its basement holds the mark that raises the water to the **middle level**.
- **Middle level:** the rooms on the west side and the dragon statue open up. More keys, and a third mark, on the middle floor, returns the water to the **high level** whenever you need it.
- **General tip:** keep toggling the boots with ↓: with them you walk along the bottom and press floor switches; without them you float to the surface. If you get stuck, check the locked doors on the other two levels: there is almost always a key behind a different height.
- **Dark Link:** beyond a misty room with an islet and a tree. He copies your every move and punishes targeted attacks. The Megaton Hammer or Din’s Fire work far better than the sword. LevelUp feels the duel loses tension in the remake.
- **Longshot:** after beating Dark Link. It doubles the Hookshot’s reach.
- **Boss Key:** beyond the whirlpool channel (walk it in the boots) and the rolling boulder room.
- **Boss door:** with the water at the high level, use the Longshot from the central room to reach the ledge by the door.

**Gold Skulltulas #70–74:** #70 at the end of the south corridor on the lowest floor (blow up the wall), #71 in the room with the diagonally sliding platforms, #72 on the wall of the whirlpool channel (with the boots and the Hookshot), #73 inside the central tower (with the Longshot) and #74 in the rolling boulder channel (with the Longshot).

## Boss: Morpha

Morpha is a mass of water with a red nucleus that moves around the room’s pool.

1. When it raises a tentacle, target the nucleus with ZL and fire the Longshot (ZR) to pull it out of the water.
2. Once on the floor, the nucleus bounces around: slash it (Y) before it gets back to the pool.
3. Do not stand against a wall with a tentacle next to you: it grabs you and throws you.

Once it falls, press A to pick up **Heart Container #6**. Ruto awakens as the Sage of Water and gives you the **Water Medallion**.

## After the temple

The lake fills up again and, in the remake, **Zora Domain thaws** after Morpha’s defeat. At dawn, shoot an arrow at the sun from the islands in the middle of the lake: a chest with the **Fire Arrows** drops (not yet confirmed for the remake). **Gold Skulltula #51** is in the tree on the warp islet. **Piece of Heart #30** is on the laboratory roof (Magic Bean or Scarecrow’s Song). At the fishing pond, catch a big fish as an adult for the **Golden Scale**, and with it dive to the bottom of the laboratory tank for **Piece of Heart #18**.

> **Don’t leave without:** the Iron Boots, the Zora Tunic, the Longshot, a spare bottle of Blue Fire and the Fire Arrows at dawn.
