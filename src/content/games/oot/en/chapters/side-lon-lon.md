> **Order:** as a child, as soon as Talon is back at the ranch (you wake him at Hyrule Castle with the Cucco that hatches from Malon’s egg); as an adult, after you win Epona.

Lon Lon Ranch sits in the middle of Hyrule Field. In the remake it looks more like Breath of the Wild and is part of the field map, with no loading screen. Talon, his daughter Malon and Ingo live there.

## Malon and Epona’s Song

Malon waits for you in the corral with the foal Epona. Talk to her and take out the Ocarina: she teaches you **Epona’s Song** (X Y A X Y A). It is the key to getting Epona as an adult (see the Epona and Horseback Archery step).

## Talon’s Super Cuccos (bottle)

1. During the day, go through the first door on the left of the ranch and talk to Talon.
2. For 10 Rupees he offers you a game: find his **three Super Cuccos** among all the Cuccos in the room within **30 seconds**.
3. Grab each Super Cucco and bring it to Talon before time runs out.
4. The first time you win he gives you a **bottle with Lon Lon Milk**. After that you can replay the game to refill it.

> 💡 According to one remake guide, the Super Cuccos puff out their chests, raise their wings and strut, while the regular ones peck at the ground. Another trick: before you start, move the regular Cuccos to one side of the room.

## The silo Piece of Heart

At the back of the ranch there is a silo (the tower-shaped storehouse). Inside, push and pull the crates until you uncover a gap in the back wall. Young Link fits through it and the **Piece of Heart** is behind it. Remake guides still list it in the same place.

There are cows in the silo and the stable: in the original game, if you play Epona’s Song to them with an empty bottle, they give you milk (not yet confirmed for the remake).

## Gold Skulltulas

The ranch has **3 Gold Skulltulas** in the remake, one fewer than in the original game, and nobody knows yet which one moved. Some only come out at night: equip the Echo Gem, which flashes and vibrates when one is nearby.

## As an adult

- Seven years later Ingo runs the ranch and Talon sleeps in a house in Kakariko Village. You wake him with the Pocket Cucco from the Biggoron Sword chain and he goes home.
- Once you win Epona, the ranch goes back to Malon. Talk to her in the corral for her **obstacle course**: two laps on horseback jumping the fences in order. If you beat her record (50 seconds in the original game), she sends a cow to your house in Kokiri Forest so you have free milk (not yet confirmed for the remake).
