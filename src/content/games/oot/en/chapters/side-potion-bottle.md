> **Order:** as a child, as soon as you reach Hyrule Castle Town after the Great Deku Tree. It is the first bottle you can get and the easiest.

The remake has **5 bottles**, one more than the original game. The new one comes from the owner of the Potion Shop in Hyrule Castle Town, a vain character who invites you to gaze upon his wares “or my own splendor”. In the remake all of Castle Town, Temple of Time included, is a single map with a free camera.

## Step by step

1. Find the Potion Shop in the row of shops on the east side of the square, next to the street up to the Temple of Time. It has a potion drawn over the door.
2. Talk to the owner: he is flustered because a bug has escaped.
3. Offer to help. He gives you an empty bottle.
4. Equip the bottle in the quick-swap bar (L/R) and catch the bug scuttling across the floor near the counter with ZR.
5. The owner keeps the bug and you keep the bottle.

One remake guide says he hands it back filled with Red Potion (not yet confirmed for the remake). The steps come from two remake guides that agree on the essentials; the exact in-game wording of the request is not documented yet.

## The five bottles

- **Hyrule Castle Town Potion Shop** (child): this one.
- **Talon’s Super Cuccos** at Lon Lon Ranch (child): you get one with Lon Lon Milk.
- **Cucco Lady** in Kakariko Village (child): bring back her 7 Cuccos.
- **Ruto’s Letter** at the bottom of Lake Hylia (child): required; you need the Silver Scale to dive down to it.
- **Poe Collector** at the Ghost Shop in Hyrule Castle Town (adult): for 5 Big Poes.

## What they are for

- A **fairy** in a bottle revives you when you run out of hearts. Take at least one to every boss.
- **Blue Fire** from the Ice Cavern thaws King Zora and clears the way; you need an empty bottle to carry it.
- **Bugs** released on soft soil make Gold Skulltulas come out: ten of the hundred are in those soil patches.
- **Big Poe** souls can only be sold bottled.
- Potions and Lon Lon Milk are also kept in bottles.

> 💡 With this one, the ranch’s, the Cuccos’ and Ruto’s Letter you reach Lord Jabu-Jabu’s Belly with four bottles.
