> **Order:** it unlocks once you have been an adult and can turn back into a child, that is after the Forest Temple. After that it is available in both eras.

The Hall of Fallen Foes is a new place in the remake: a dream hall with statues of every enemy you have defeated. It replaces the 3DS version’s Boss Challenge. Nintendo Mexico’s page describes it in Spanish as “el coliseo de los monstruos” (the monster colosseum); the official in-game Spanish name is not verified yet.

## How to unlock it

1. Once you can switch eras, go to Kakariko Village as a child from Hyrule Field.
2. Just before you enter, Kaepora Gaebora stops you and explains how the hall works.
3. Find a bed or bedroll that glows green. The owl points you to the nearest one, in the south of Kakariko.
4. Choose to sleep and dream: you wake up in the Hall of Fallen Foes.

From then on you enter from any green-glowing bed or bedroll, as a child or as an adult. To leave, talk to Kaepora Gaebora.

## What’s inside

- **Enemy statues.** Every defeated enemy has a statue with a description and the places it appears. It works as a bestiary.
- **Boss rematches.** The boss statues, around the outer edge of the hall, let you fight them again. A timer records your best time and the statue tells you which items work against each one.
- **Boss gauntlets.** Several bosses in a row with a limited number of hearts and items. They pay out Rupees that you spend on upgrades inside the hall.
- **Trophies.** When you clear a gauntlet, Kaepora Gaebora delivers a trophy to your house in Kokiri Forest.

It all happens in a dream: if you die or use up items in a rematch, you lose nothing when you return to the adventure. Try strategies without fear.

## Bosses and mini-bosses

All ten bosses have a statue: Gohma, King Dodongo, Barinade, Phantom Ganon, Volvagia, Morpha, Bongo Bongo, Twinrova, Ganondorf and Ganon. The mini-bosses include Big Octo, Dark Link, Dead Hand, Flare Dancer, the Iron Knuckles, Stalfos, Lizalfos, Dinolfos, White Wolfos, the four Poe sisters, the composer brothers and the Great Moblin of the Sacred Grove.

## Statue hints

The descriptions give useful tips for the rematch:

- **King Dodongo:** bombs.
- **Volvagia:** its fire breath stops if you freeze its mouth (Ice Arrows).
- **Morpha:** its liquid tentacles are vulnerable to heat.
- **Bongo Bongo:** freezing its hands stops it from drumming.

> 💡 One review calls the hall’s rewards useless. Play it for the challenge and the times, not for the prizes.
