The last temple lies across the desert, beyond the Gerudo lands. The Spirit Temple is played in two halves: one as a child and one as an adult, linked by the Requiem of Spirit.

> **Order:** Gerudo Valley → Gerudo’s Fortress: rescue the four carpenters → Gerudo Pass → Mirage Desert → Desert Colossus → Requiem of Spirit → as a child: Silver Gauntlets → as an adult: Mirror Shield → Twinrova → Spirit Medallion.

## Gerudo Valley

The valley bridge is broken. Jump the gorge at a gallop on Epona or cross with the Longshot. By the carpenters’ tent are **Gold Skulltula #83** (behind the tent) and **#84** (on the rock arch by the first bridge, with the Hookshot). **#85 and #86** and **Pieces of Heart #21 and #22** are child ones (chapter 5).

## Gerudo’s Fortress

The Gerudo capture you as soon as they spot you and lock you in a cell; escape through the window with the Hookshot. In the remake the fortress has new fences and vines to climb, and the free camera helps you watch the patrols.

You have to rescue **four carpenters**, each in a cell guarded by a sword-wielding Gerudo. Beat the guard so she drops the key: block her strikes and counter. Once you free the fourth, the Gerudo accept you and give you **Gerudo Pass**. The carpenters go back to the valley and repair the bridge.

With the card:

- **Piece of Heart #33:** Horseback Archery on Epona. The original asked for 1,000 points; the remake’s threshold is not confirmed.
- **Piece of Heart #34:** the chest on the fortress roof, with the Longshot or the Scarecrow’s Song.
- **Gold Skulltulas #87** (on the wall above the roof, at night, with the Hookshot) **and #88** (on the farthest target of the archery range, with the Hookshot).
- **Gerudo Proving Grounds:** the fortress’s optional dungeon. The prize is the **Ice Arrows**. The Silver Rupees are silver gems that fly into the lock; the remake’s rooms are not documented yet.

## Mirage Desert

A Gerudo opens the gate to the Mirage Desert. First cross the river of quicksand with the Longshot or the Hover Boots. Then follow the flags through the sandstorm to the stone building, where **Gold Skulltula #89** is. From there, put on the Lenses of Truth (↑) to see the guiding Poe and follow it to the Desert Colossus. The remake’s exact layout is not documented (not yet confirmed).

## Desert Colossus

Sheik teaches you the **Requiem of Spirit** (L R L A R L), which warps you here. The Colossus Great Fairy, behind a bombable wall, gives you Nayru’s Love (offering not yet confirmed). **Gold Skulltulas #90 and #91** are on the southern palm tree and on the rocky mound in the middle (with the Longshot). **#92** is a child one, in the soft soil by the temple entrance. Plant a bean as a child as well for **Piece of Heart #35**.

## Spirit Temple: the child half

Turn back into a child, play the Requiem and go in. Nabooru asks you to fetch the **Silver Gauntlets** through a gap only a child fits through. Bring the Slingshot, the Boomerang and Bombchus. The layout is the base game’s (not yet confirmed for the remake).

- Push on past Armos, Keese and Stalfos, collect the silver gems to open the doors and use a Bombchu on the wall that calls for one.
- The exit leads onto the right hand of the great statue, outside the temple: the **Silver Gauntlets** are there. When you take them, Koume and Kotake kidnap Nabooru.

**Child Skulltulas:** **#93** in the room with the iron gate, **#94** in the vertical shaft leading to the Bombchu wall room and **#95** in the corridor before the Iron Knuckle room. All three are collected with the Boomerang.

## Spirit Temple: the adult half

Grow up again and go in with the Silver Gauntlets: shove aside the big block at the entrance. The temple now revolves around sunlight and the **Mirror Shield**.

- **Iron Knuckle:** hits hard but is slow. In the remake **you can block its axe swings with your shield**. Hit it and step away; once it loses its armour it gets faster.
- **Mirror Shield:** on the statue’s left hand, after beating the last Iron Knuckle, who turns out to be a brainwashed Nabooru. It reflects light and, in the remake, also **Beamos lasers**.
- **Light puzzles:** hold up the Mirror Shield to aim sunbeams at the sun symbols on the walls and open doors. If a big mirror can be turned, rotate it until the beam reaches the symbol.
- **Great statue:** climb through the goddess room, collect the **Boss Key** and, at the end, reflect light onto the statue’s face to open the way to the boss.

**Adult Skulltulas:** **#96** behind the block in the rolling boulder room (play the Song of Time) and **#97** on the wall next to the great statue (with the Longshot).

## Boss: Twinrova

**Phase 1:** Koume (fire) and Kotake (ice) fly around the room. Hold up the Mirror Shield to send one sister’s beam at the other: Kotake’s ice hurts Koume and Koume’s fire hurts Kotake. Keep it up until they merge.

**Phase 2:** they fuse into Twinrova. Absorb **three shots of the same element** in a row with the shield and release the charge facing her. If a shot of the other element hits you, the charge is lost: dodge that one. When she drops, stunned, hit her with the sword. The first guides say her attacks were toned down in the remake (not yet confirmed).

Once she falls, press A to pick up **Heart Container #8**. Nabooru awakens as the Sage of Spirit and gives you the **Spirit Medallion**.

> **Don’t leave without:** the Silver Gauntlets (with them you can now collect Gold Skulltula #46 at Zora Fountain), the Mirror Shield, Gerudo Pass and, if you are going for everything, the Ice Arrows from the Gerudo Proving Grounds.
