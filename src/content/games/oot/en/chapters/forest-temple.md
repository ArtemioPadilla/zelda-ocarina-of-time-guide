Sheik has sent you to the forest, but the Forest Temple sits high up a tree you can only reach with the Hookshot. Before you go, two stops: the Kakariko graveyard and Lon Lon Ranch. The temple is the one the remake changed most, so this part of the chapter relies on the little that has been documented.

> **Order:** Lon Lon Ranch (Epona) → graveyard: Dampé’s race (Hookshot) → windmill (Song of Storms) → Kokiri Forest → Lost Woods → Sacred Grove (Minuet of the Forest) → Forest Temple → the four Poe sisters → Phantom Ganon → Forest Medallion.

## Epona

At Lon Lon Ranch, Ingo has taken over. Pay to ride, call the mare by playing **Epona’s Song** (X Y A X Y A) and beat Ingo in the races. In the end he bets everything: win again and jump the fence to keep **Epona** (no remake changes confirmed). From now on you call her with the song in almost any outdoor area, and you need her for Gerudo Valley and for the field’s Big Poes, now marked by purple lanterns.

## The Hookshot and the Song of Storms

In the graveyard, pull back the headstone of Dampé’s grave (A) and go down. His ghost challenges you to a race through a tunnel of fire: follow him without losing sight of him and jump (X) over the flames. At the end he gives you the **Hookshot**. Run the race again in under a minute to win **Piece of Heart #24**.

The race exit drops you inside the Kakariko windmill, on the platform with **Piece of Heart #25**. Below, Guru-Guru, the man with the barrel organ, demands a song: play the Ocarina and you learn the **Song of Storms** (L R X L R X). It is used for **Gold Skulltula #10**, the Zora’s River frogs and draining the village well as a child (chapter 9).

With the Hookshot you can now collect **Gold Skulltula #20** (roof of the house in the south of the village) and **#7** in Kokiri Forest. The graveyard’s **Piece of Heart #23** is on a ledge you reach riding the Magic Bean plant you sowed as a child.

## Kokiri Forest and the Sacred Grove

The forest is overrun with monsters. In the Lost Woods, Mido will not let you through to the grove until you play him **Saria’s Song**. If you planted a bean there as a child, the plant takes you to **Gold Skulltula #23**.

The Sacred Grove is patrolled by spear Moblins: slip past them along the maze’s side passages. At the end the **Great Moblin** blocks the way with his club: stay clear of the shockwaves and look for the gap to get past (strategy not yet confirmed for the remake). **Gold Skulltula #56** is at the top of the maze’s east wall.

At the stump, Sheik teaches you the **Minuet of the Forest** (L X Y A Y A), which warps you here. Hookshot onto the tree branch to reach the entrance.

## Forest Temple

Nintendo kept the temple’s mechanisms, but it is the dungeon with the most documented changes: there is a **new shortcut lift in the north-west room**, the block-sliding puzzle **no longer has a time limit** (but you have to pick the right pieces), and one doorway is **blocked by rocks**, so the route differs from the original. Chest and key positions in this section are from the base game (not yet confirmed for the remake).

- **Entrance:** two Wolfos wait in the first courtyard. Hit them from behind or when they attack. **Gold Skulltula #57** is on the vines on the east side.
- **Main hall:** four torches and a lift in the middle. The four Poe sisters (Jo, Beth, Amy and Meg) steal the flames and hide around the temple: you have to defeat them all to go down to the basement. In the remake **Jo is a deep, vivid red**. **Gold Skulltula #58** is on a wall of the hall.
- **East and west courtyards:** Octoroks, Deku Babas and a well. Get your first small keys here; the well water is drained from a switch on the upper floors. **Gold Skulltulas #59 and #60** are in the north-east and north-west rooms of the lower floor.
- **Fairy Bow:** after beating a pair of Stalfos in the west wing. Aim with ZR, in third person and, if you like, with motion controls. You use it on eye switches and the Poe paintings.
- **Jo and Beth:** each hides in a painting. Shoot the painting with an arrow while she is visible and defeat the Poe that comes out. Target her with ZL and strike when she closes in.
- **Amy:** complete her portrait on the floor by pushing blocks. In the remake there is no timer, but not every piece fits: keep trying until they do.
- **Twisted corridor:** shoot the eye switch to straighten it and push on to the **Boss Key**. Watch out for the falling ceiling in the tiled room and for the Floormasters, which split into three.
- **Meg:** once you return the three flames, Meg appears in the main hall and splits into four copies. The real one spins for a moment when they appear: shoot that one.
- **Basement:** the central lift goes down. Push the walls to turn the circular corridor until the boss door is within reach. **Gold Skulltula #61** is there.

## Boss: Phantom Ganon

**Phase 1:** Phantom Ganon rides inside the gallery’s paintings. Two riders appear: the fake turns back and the real one bursts out of the painting. Shoot him with the Bow just as he comes out. After several hits he comes down to fight.

**Phase 2:** he floats and throws energy balls. Bat them back with the sword (Y) at the right moment and keep the rally going until one hits him: he falls to the ground and you can slash him. The first guides say his projectiles are slower in the remake (not yet confirmed). If he fires his lightning, dash out of the way (hold A).

Once he falls, press A to pick up **Heart Container #4**. Saria awakens as the Sage of the Forest and gives you the **Forest Medallion**.

## After the temple

The Deku Tree Sprout tells you the truth about the Kokiri. Go back to the Temple of Time: Sheik teaches you the **Prelude of Light** (X A X A Y X) to return there from anywhere.

> **Don’t leave without:** the Hookshot, Epona, the Song of Storms, Piece of Heart #24 (race in under a minute) and Gold Skulltulas #7 and #20, which you can now reach.
