> **Order:** three as a child (with bombs) and three as an adult. Start with the summit of Death Mountain: without the Magic Meter there are no spells and no magic arrows.

At each fountain, stand on the Triforce symbol and play Zelda’s Lullaby (Y X A Y X A). The Great Fairy gives you her gift and, on later visits, refills your hearts and magic. The entrances are hidden behind cracked walls or rocks, which in the remake are visible to the naked eye.

## What’s new: offerings

In the remake each Great Fairy asks for a Rupee offering before she gives you her gift, and the amount goes up with each fountain. One guide gives these figures for the usual order: 25, 50, 100, 200, 300 and 500, that is 1,175 Rupees in total. The same guide warns that the price may depend on how many fountains you have already visited (figures not yet confirmed for the remake). For the 300 and 500 offerings you need the Adult’s Wallet (10 Gold Skulltulas, holds 500).

## The six fountains

1. **Summit of Death Mountain (child).** After climbing the Skulltula wall, bomb the wall to the left of Kaepora Gaebora. You get the **Magic Meter** and **Din’s Fire**, which in the remake plays out in real time.
2. **Hyrule Castle (child).** Go past the gatehouse and, where the path turns left, bomb the rock on the corner. You get the **improved Spin Attack**, which sends out a shockwave (it shows up in the Collection menu as the Gift of Improved Spin).
3. **Zora Fountain (child).** Bomb the wall in the southern corner. You get **Farore’s Wind**. In the remake it costs 2 magic (was 6), takes you back to the exact spot you marked, and that spot can only be replaced, not dispelled.
4. **Death Mountain Crater (adult).** Cross the bridge south of the exit to Goron City and smash the rocks at the cave on the right with the Megaton Hammer. You get **doubled magic** (Gift of Doubled Magic).
5. **Desert Colossus.** Bomb the cracked wall on the north side. You get **Nayru’s Love**, a barrier that protects you for a while.
6. **Outside Ganondorf’s Castle (adult).** Get the Golden Gauntlets from the castle’s Shadow trial, come back out, turn left after the bridge and lift the pillar at the end. You get **doubled defense** (Gift of Halved Damage): you take half damage.

## CONFLICT: where is Din’s Fire?

Zelda Wiki and two remake guides agree: Din’s Fire and the improved Spin Attack **swapped fountains** compared with the original game. Din’s Fire moves to the summit of Death Mountain and the Spin Attack to Hyrule Castle, because the improved spin no longer uses magic. Two other guides still give the old layout (Din’s Fire at the castle), but they look copied from the Nintendo 64 version.

In practice: visit both child fountains and you will leave with both gifts. If an old video sends you to the castle for Din’s Fire, it is about the original game.

> 💡 With doubled defense and all 36 Pieces of Heart you reach the end with 20 hearts that last twice as long. Leave this fountain for just before the final battle.
