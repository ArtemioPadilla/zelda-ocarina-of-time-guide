## Walkthrough

You wake up with Navi, who leads you to speak with the Great Deku Tree: he has just asked you to lift the curse that is killing him. Before heading in, explore Kokiri Forest: Mido blocks the path until you have a sword and shield; the Kokiri Shop sells the **Deku Shield** and Deku Nuts; and the hidden grove behind the hollow log holds a chest with the **Kokiri Sword**, essential before entering the dungeon.

Inside the Deku Tree, fight through Deku Babas and Gohma Larvae. The **Fairy Slingshot** turns up in a chest partway through: light distant torches with it and knock down webs to reveal chests and Skulltulas. The circular vine room lifts you several floors; follow the web platforms up to the boss room.

> 💡 Shoot the Deku Babas with the Slingshot before closing in: it saves you several hits.

## Boss: Queen Gohma

Wait for her eye to open and hit it with the Slingshot to stun her; rush in and slash while she is down. Watch for the larvae dropping from the ceiling: a Deku Nut clears them all at once.

Defeating her gets you the **Kokiri’s Emerald**, and the dying Great Deku Tree tells you the truth about Ganondorf before he passes.
