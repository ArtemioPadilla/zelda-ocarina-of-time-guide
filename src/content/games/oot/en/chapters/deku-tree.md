The adventure opens with a voiced narration and the first piece of the Threads of Time tapestry: the Great Deku Tree sends Navi to wake Link, the only Kokiri without a fairy. This chapter is your tutorial: you learn the new controls, gear up and clear the first dungeon.

> **Order:** wake up with Navi → Deku Shield from the shop → Kokiri Sword in the training area → Mido lets you through → Great Deku Tree → Gohma → Kokiri Emerald → Fairy Ocarina.

## Controls you will use from the start

The remake changes the control layout compared with any older guide:

- **X** jumps anywhere: there is no more automatic jump at the edge of a platform. It also climbs low ledges.
- **Hold A** to dash (the old roll button). **Y** is the sword; hold and release it for the spin attack.
- **ZL** targets and **ZR** uses your single equipped item. **L** and **R** open the horizontal bar to swap items on the fly.
- The D-pad is fixed: **←** Ocarina, **↑** headgear, **↓** footwear and **→** Navi. Navi no longer interrupts you: she talks when you call her.
- The camera is free on the right stick, and aiming switches to a third-person view (buttons or motion controls, set in the options).

## Kokiri Forest: sword and shield

Mido blocks the way to the Great Deku Tree until you carry a sword and shield. Collect Rupees by cutting grass and lifting rocks (the starting wallet holds 200) and buy the **Deku Shield** at the Kokiri Shop. Keep in mind that shops charge more in the remake than before.

The **Kokiri Sword** is in a chest in the training area: crawl through the hole in the wall by the small cliff, dodge the boulder rolling through the maze and open the chest at the back. Equip both in the Equipment menu and go back to Mido.

Grab the first overworld Skulltula while you are here: **Gold Skulltula #5** comes out at night behind the Know-It-All Brothers’ house. In the remake you can sleep in your bed to change the time, so there is no need to wait. **Gold Skulltula #6** is in the soft soil by the shop and needs bugs from a bottle, and **#7** is an adult one (on the roof of the Twins’ house, with the Hookshot): note them for later.

## The Great Deku Tree

The first dungeon keeps its vertical layout: a big hollow trunk with a web on the lobby floor and side rooms on every level. The exact position of some chests may have changed (not yet confirmed for the remake).

- **Ground floor:** clear the Deku Babas (Big Deku Babas no longer exist in the remake; they are all normal-sized). Pick up Deku Sticks from the ones you defeat. The floor web will not give way yet.
- **Second floor:** climb the vines on the wall. A Deku Scrub waits in the side room: hold up your shield to bounce its nuts back and talk to it once you catch it. Further on is the **Fairy Slingshot**. In the remake the floating platform in this area is now a **wooden bridge that collapses**: dash across with A without stopping. The opening of the target you shoot with the Slingshot also moved (not yet confirmed in detail).
- **Map and Compass:** the Map is on the lower floors and the Compass in a third-floor room, where **Gold Skulltula #1** also sits on the wall.
- **Breaking the web:** from the top of the trunk, jump (X) onto the central web in the lobby to break through it and drop into the basement. **Gold Skulltula #2** is there, on the vines by the water.
- **Basement:** light a Deku Stick on a torch and burn the web blocking the way. Shoot the eye switch with the Slingshot to open the bars: **Gold Skulltula #3** is behind them. Push the block, climb up and continue to the room with three Deku Scrubs: hit them in the order the first one told you (2, 3, 1) to open the boss door.

**Gold Skulltula #4** is behind a bombable wall in the basement room where the Gohma Larvae hatch. You need bombs and the Boomerang, so come back once you have them.

## Boss: Gohma

When you walk in, look up: Gohma is on the ceiling. In the remake, **while she is immune she covers her eye with her front legs**, so do not waste seeds while it is covered. When she uncovers it and it turns red:

1. Aim the Slingshot (ZR) and shoot the eye. She drops to the floor, stunned.
2. Dash at her holding A and slash the eye with Y until she gets up.
3. When she lays eggs, burst the larvae before they grow. A Deku Nut stuns them all.

Repeat the cycle two or three times. If she climbs to the ceiling, follow her shadow with the free camera and wait for her to drop.

Defeating her drops **Heart Container #1**: in the remake you pick it up by pressing A; touching it is no longer enough. Leave through the blue circle.

## After the dungeon

The Great Deku Tree gives you the **Kokiri Emerald**, tells you about Ganondorf and dies. In the remake his withered leaves rain down over the whole forest, the Kokiri react and Kokiri Forest stays silent, without music, until you leave.

On your way out, Saria says goodbye on the bridge and gives you the **Fairy Ocarina**. From now on you play it with D-pad ← and the notes are A, X, Y, L and R. If you prefer, you can hum the songs into the microphone.

> **Don’t leave without:** the Deku Shield and Kokiri Sword equipped, and Gold Skulltula #5 if you have already spent a night. Numbers #4, #6 and #7 stay pending for later.
