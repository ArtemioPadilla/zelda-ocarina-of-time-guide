With the Kokiri Emerald, the Goron Ruby and the Zora Sapphire, head back to Castle Town to bring them to Zelda. You arrive too late: this is the end of Link’s childhood and the jump to adult Link.

> **Order:** castle gate → Zelda flees and Ganondorf confronts you → Ocarina of Time in the moat → Song of Time → Temple of Time altar → Door of Time → Master Sword → Rauru and the Light Medallion → adult Link.

## Before you grow up: what only child Link does

You can turn back into a child whenever you like by returning the sword to its pedestal, so none of this is lost. Still, it is worth doing now rather than going back and forth later:

- **Pieces of Heart #19 and #20:** both need time travel. The password for the #19 Business Scrub and the hint for the #20 chest are read as an adult from Fairy Thoughts, but the piece is collected as a child. Note them for your first trip back to childhood.
- **Plant Magic Beans** in every patch of soft soil. As an adult, those plants are platforms that take you to several Pieces of Heart (#11, #23, #32 and #35) and to **Gold Skulltula #23**.
- **Child Skulltulas:** the ones that come out at night in the village, the ranch and the river, and the soft-soil ones that need bugs, such as #6, #21, #22, #24 and #47. The remake’s map marks what you are missing in each area.
- **Gerudo Valley as a child:** **Pieces of Heart #21 and #22** (with a Cucco from the bridge) and **Gold Skulltulas #85 and #86**.
- **Death Mountain Crater as a child:** in the remake a Business Scrub sells a Piece of Heart for 100 Rupees; it is the one that used to be in the Ice Cavern. You reach it through Darunia’s room in Goron City (details in chapter 7).

## Zelda flees and the Ocarina of Time

As you approach the castle drawbridge, Impa gallops out with Zelda. Zelda throws something into the moat just before Ganondorf catches up and knocks you down. Jump into the water where it fell and pick up the **Ocarina of Time**. In the remake both this and the Fairy Ocarina hang visibly from Link’s belt.

Picking it up gives you a vision of Zelda, who teaches you the **Song of Time** (A L R A L R). Besides opening the Door of Time, it moves the blue time blocks you will see in several dungeons.

## The Temple of Time

The temple is part of the same Castle Town map, with no loading screen. At the altar, place the three Spiritual Stones and play the Song of Time: the Door of Time opens.

On the other side the **Master Sword** waits in its pedestal, redesigned in the remake in the style of Skyward Sword. **Save before you draw it**: the remake has a single manual save per console user plus several autosaves, and a lot of story happens at once when you take the sword.

When you draw it, in the remake Link opens a spectral path to the Sacred Realm. Ganondorf, who was following you, freezes him and walks in first. The sword seals Link away for seven years.

## Waking up as an adult

You wake in the Chamber of Sages. Rauru, the Sage of Light, explains what happened and gives you the **Light Medallion**. Back in the temple Sheik appears, tells you about the five temples and sends you to the forest.

When you step outside for the first time, Link and Navi turn towards Hyrule Castle and see Ganondorf’s Castle in its place. Castle Town is now a ruin full of **ReDeads**, far more than in the original, even on the road to the drawbridge. With the free camera they can freeze you with their scream, and they charge at you if you get close. Go around them at a distance or freeze them with the Sun’s Song.

## What is new for adult Link

- The adult green tunic is now called the **Hero’s Clothes**. The Kokiri Sword and the Slingshot are child items; you now use the Master Sword, and the inventory separates child, adult and shared items.
- Masks can be used as an adult too.
- The Deku Shield no longer fits: equip the Hylian Shield.
- **Time travel:** put the sword back in its pedestal to become a child, and draw it again to grow up. After the Forest Temple, Sheik teaches you the Prelude of Light, which brings you here from anywhere.

## The Hall of Fallen Foes

This new area unlocks now: after first reaching the adult era, return to Kakariko Village **as a child** and talk to Kaepora Gaebora. From then on, resting in any bed or bedroll lets you enter this dream gallery to rematch the bosses and mini-bosses you have defeated, with a timer and gauntlet challenges that pay out Rupees. The rewards do not matter much, but the statues double as a bestiary.

## Next step

Sheik waits for you in the forest. Before the Forest Temple you need the **Hookshot**, won as an adult in Dampé’s race in the Kakariko graveyard, and you will want to get **Epona** back at Lon Lon Ranch. Both steps are in the next chapter.

> **Don’t leave without:** saving before you take the Master Sword and planting the Magic Beans. As soon as you are an adult, read the Fairy Thoughts for Pieces of Heart #19 and #20 and come back as a child for them.
