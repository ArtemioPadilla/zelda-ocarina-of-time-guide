> **Order:** the Piece of Heart as a child, as soon as you reach Lake Hylia; the Golden Scale as an adult; remake guides place it after the Water Temple.

The Fishing Pond is the little house by the water at Lake Hylia, which in the remake is part of the Hyrule Field map. The owner rents you a rod and you can fish for as long as you like. You can keep or release each fish you land, but you only keep one at a time: show it to the owner so he can measure it.

## What changes in the remake

- Renting costs **30 Rupees** according to Nintendo Life (20 in the original game).
- Fish are measured in **centimetres** in the remake guides, not pounds, so old guides’ figures won’t help you.

## Piece of Heart (child)

Catch a fish of about **55 cm or more** and the owner gives you a **Piece of Heart**. Two remake guides give that figure and Nintendo Life got it with a 59 cm fish. The biggest fish tend to be in the middle of the pond.

## Golden Scale (adult)

As an adult, an even bigger fish gets you the **Golden Scale**, which lets you dive deeper and for longer. One remake guide asks for more than **65 cm** (not yet confirmed for the remake).

With the Golden Scale, go into the Lake Hylia Lakeside Laboratory, on the north side of the lake, and dive to the bottom of the pool without the Iron Boots: the scientist gives you another **Piece of Heart**.

## How to fish

1. Pay the owner and pick up the rod.
2. Cast the lure near a fish, reel in slowly and wait for a bite.
3. When it bites, hook it and reel in without letting it get away.
4. Bring the fish to the owner. If it breaks the record, it goes on display in the tank by the entrance with your name.

> ⚠️ In the original game a sinking lure was hidden in the pond. It is against the rules: if you land a record fish with it, the owner refuses the prize and the tank marks it as cheating (not yet confirmed for the remake).

The remake’s exact rod controls are not documented yet; follow the on-screen prompts.
