With all six medallions, go back to the Temple of Time. This is the beginning of the end: from here on everything leads to Ganondorf, so now is the time to wrap up whatever you have left.

> **Order:** Temple of Time: Sheik and the Light Arrows → Zelda is kidnapped → the Sages’ bridge → outer Great Fairy (Golden Gauntlets) → six trials → tower → Ganondorf.

## Zelda and the Light Arrows

In the Temple of Time, Sheik reveals herself as **Princess Zelda**. She tells you what happened during the seven years and gives you the **Light Arrows**, the only weapon that truly hurts Ganondorf. Right then, Ganondorf traps her in a crystal and carries her off to his castle.

## Before you cross: wrap up what is left

The remake has **no New Game+, no Hero Mode and no Master Quest**, and there is only one manual save per console user plus several autosaves. If you want 100%, do it now and do not overwrite your manual save with one from after the ending:

- **All 100 Skulltulas** (the 100-token reward is a repeatable 200-Rupee Gold Rupee) and the **36 Pieces of Heart**. The remake’s map marks what is missing in each area, and the Echo Gem blinks near Skulltulas.
- **The Biggoron trading sequence**, the **Big Poes** (5 get you a bottle and all 10 a Gold Rupee), the **Great Fairies** and the **Ice Arrows** from the Gerudo Proving Grounds.
- **The Hall of Fallen Foes**, to revisit defeated bosses if it interests you.

## The bridge and the outer Great Fairy

Go to where Hyrule Castle used to stand. In front of the lava moat, the six Sages form a bridge of light to Ganondorf’s Castle.

To the left of the path, a black pillar blocks a Great Fairy Fountain. Once you have the **Golden Gauntlets** (you get them inside, in the Shadow trial), lift it: the fairy halves the damage you take. **Gold Skulltula #98** is in this area; see its entry.

## The six trials

In the central room, a six-coloured barrier protects the tower. Each door leads to one Sage’s trial. At the end of each is an orb: shoot it with a **Light Arrow** to dissolve that part of the barrier. The remake keeps the six trials, but their rooms are not documented yet; this is the base game’s design (not yet confirmed for the remake). Carry fairies and potions in your five bottles: there are no shops inside.

Recommended order, because the Golden Gauntlets from the Shadow trial open two others:

1. **Shadow:** invisible platforms over the void. Put on the Lenses of Truth (↑) and the Hover Boots (↓); light the torch with a Fire Arrow or Din’s Fire to make the path appear, and use the Megaton Hammer where needed. The **Golden Gauntlets** are here.
2. **Forest:** wind from fans, Wolfos and silver gems. The Iron Boots keep you grounded and Fire Arrows burn away what blocks the way.
3. **Water:** Freezards and red ice. Fill a bottle with Blue Fire inside the trial itself, melt the ice and pound the rusted switch with the Hammer.
4. **Spirit:** send a Bombchu along the wall to get past the first obstacle, collect the silver gems and use the Mirror Shield to aim light at the sun symbol.
5. **Fire:** silver gems over lava, with the Goron Tunic and the Hover Boots. In the remake lava kills on contact: do not risk last-second jumps. At the end, lift the pillar with the Golden Gauntlets.
6. **Light:** lift the black rock blocking the door with the Golden Gauntlets. Inside, play Zelda’s Lullaby where needed and use the Lenses to see what is hidden.

## The tower

With the barrier gone, climb the tower stairs. Each floor is a sealed room with tough enemies (Dinolfos, Stalfos, Iron Knuckles): defeat them all to open the door. In the remake you can block Iron Knuckle axe swings with your shield. The **Boss Key** is on the way up (floor not yet confirmed for the remake). Gather hearts and magic before the last staircase. As you climb you will hear Ganondorf’s organ.

## Boss: Ganondorf

Ganondorf floats above a central platform surrounded by tiles.

1. **Rally:** he throws an energy ball. Bat it back with the sword (Y) at the right moment and keep going until it hits him.
2. **Light Arrow:** while he hangs stunned in the air, shoot him with a Light Arrow (ZR). He drops onto the central platform.
3. **On the ground:** jump (X) to his platform and land as many slashes as you can before he gets up.
4. **Special attacks:** sometimes he fires a burst of orbs that cannot be returned: dash away (hold A) or use the spin attack. When he slams the floor he breaks tiles; if you fall, climb back up the edges.

Reviews mention slightly tweaked patterns for some bosses without detail (not yet confirmed for Ganondorf). Defeating him does not quite finish him: the story continues in the next chapter.

> **Don’t leave without:** whatever 100% you want, a manual save made before crossing the bridge, the Golden Gauntlets and the outer Great Fairy, and bottles full of fairies before you climb the tower.
