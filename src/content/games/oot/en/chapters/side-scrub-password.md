> **Order:** you need bombs (Dodongo Cavern) and to have reached the adult era. You find the Business Scrub as a child, read the password as an adult and buy the piece as a child.

In the original game a Business Scrub in a Hyrule Field grotto, near the Lake Hylia fence, sold a Piece of Heart for 10 Rupees. The remake turns it into a small time-travel puzzle: the Scrub is now by Hyrule Castle, asks for a password and charges **100 Rupees**.

## Step by step

1. **Find the hole (child).** Leave Hyrule Castle Town over the entrance bridge. Just to the right, next to the river, there is a patch of cracked ground; in the remake hidden grottos look like this. Blow it open with a bomb and jump in.
2. **Talk to the Scrub.** It spits Deku Nuts at you: bounce them back with your shield to stun it, then talk to it. It says it only sells to those who know the password. For now there is nothing else to do.
3. **Read the password (adult).** Come back to the Castle Town entrance as an adult. On the tree opposite the hole a small symbol glows: it is a Fairy Thought, a note Navi reads for you. It reveals the password: “Sell something, will ya?”.
4. **Don’t buy as an adult.** If you go into the grotto as an adult and give the password, the Scrub tells you it is sold out.
5. **Buy as a child.** Turn back into a child at the Temple of Time, go down into the grotto, give the password and buy the **Piece of Heart** for 100 Rupees. The remake’s starting wallet holds 200, so you can afford it without upgrades.

> 💡 Save up before you go down: in the remake shops are pricier and there are more ways to spend.

## Another new Scrub: the Crater

The Ice Cavern’s Piece of Heart is no longer there. Another Business Scrub sells it in Death Mountain Crater, also as a child and also for 100 Rupees. Enter the Crater from the top of the trail, head left and go down towards the climbing net. Bounce its seed back with the Deku Shield and talk to it. The heat drains your health right away, so go in with a fairy in a bottle or plenty of hearts.

## Business Scrub Seeds

The remake adds a new consumable, the Business Scrub Seed. You find it in new chests around the world and with some amiibo. Using one summons a wandering Business Scrub wherever you are, selling Red Potion, Green Potion, a bug, five bombs or five Deku Nuts. It is very handy mid-dungeon when you run out of bombs.
