Impa sends you to look for the second Spiritual Stone on Death Mountain. The road goes through Kakariko Village, takes a detour through the Lost Woods for the song Darunia wants, and ends in Dodongo’s Cavern, the bomb dungeon.

> **Order:** Kakariko Village → Death Mountain Trail → Goron City (Darunia won’t listen) → Lost Woods → Sacred Grove (Saria’s Song) → Darunia and the Goron Bracelet → Dodongo’s Cavern → King Dodongo → Goron Ruby.

## Kakariko Village

The village has a new layout in the remake, so do not trust old maps: use the in-game map, which also marks which collectibles you are missing in each area.

- **House of Skulltula:** the cursed family rewards you for every 10 tokens: 10 = Adult’s Wallet (500 Rupees), 20 = **Echo Gem** (detects nearby Skulltulas; replaces the Stone of Agony), 30 = Giant’s Wallet (1,000), 40 = Bombchus, 50 = **Piece of Heart #36** and 100 = a Gold Rupee (200) every time.
- **The Cucco Lady:** round up her seven Cuccos and she gives you a bottle (no remake changes confirmed).
- **Gold Skulltulas #15–19:** they come out at night around the village; **#20** is an adult one.
- **Piece of Heart #7:** the owl at the mountain summit drops you into the village, near a ledge with a cow; see its entry.

**Graveyard:** Dampé offers his grave-digging tour (**Piece of Heart #8**), faster in the remake at 10 Rupees per dig. **#9** is in the grave at the back; according to Nintendo Life you no longer need the Sun’s Song for the ReDead (not yet confirmed). In the royal tomb at the back of the graveyard, play Zelda’s Lullaby in front of the headstone with the Triforce, go down and read the wall at the end: you learn the **Sun’s Song** (A R X A R X), which switches between day and night and freezes ReDeads. **Gold Skulltulas #24** (soft soil, with bugs) **and #25** (on a wall, with the Boomerang) are here.

## Death Mountain Trail and Goron City

Show Zelda’s Letter to the guard at the north gate and climb. The mountain has been redesigned: dodge the boulders rolling down and stay away from Bomb Flowers unless you need them. The cavern entrance is blocked by a huge rock.

Goron City has new fences and vines to climb. Play Zelda’s Lullaby in front of Darunia’s door on the ground floor. The Goron chief is in a terrible mood and only wants to hear “the music of the forest”: you are missing Saria’s Song.

## Lost Woods and Sacred Grove

Go back to Kokiri Forest and enter the Lost Woods. Follow the melody: it gets louder when you pick the right clearing. Mido greets you at the entrance; in the remake he is a far kinder character.

- **Piece of Heart #1:** play Saria’s Song to the Skull Kid on the stump (come back once you learn it).
- **Piece of Heart #2:** win the Skull Kids’ memory game. In the remake the notes appear on screen and rhythm does not matter (not fully confirmed).
- **Gold Skulltulas #21 and #22:** in the soft soil of two clearings, with bugs. **#23** needs a Magic Bean and a return as an adult.

In the Sacred Grove a Wolfos waits at the entrance of the maze. At the far end, Saria teaches you **Saria’s Song** (R A Y R A Y). Besides opening the way with Darunia, it lets you talk to her from afar.

## Darunia and the Goron Bracelet

Play Saria’s Song for Darunia: he starts dancing and gives you the **Goron Bracelet**, which lets you pull up Bomb Flowers. Now you can blow up the rock at the cavern entrance with one.

In the city are **Piece of Heart #10** (light the torches so the central pot spins, and throw a bomb in when it shows the smiling face) and **Gold Skulltula #35** (in a crate in the north-west room, after blowing up the rocks at the door). On the trail, **#26** is in the soft soil at the cavern entrance and **#27** is behind a bombable wall higher up; you need the Boomerang to bring back the token. **#28 and #29** need the Megaton Hammer as an adult. **Piece of Heart #11** is on the ledge above the cavern entrance: in the remake you can no longer reach it with jumps, so plant a Magic Bean (sold on Zora’s River) in the soft soil next to it and come back as an adult.

## Dodongo’s Cavern

The whole dungeon revolves around Bomb Flowers: pull them up (A), throw them at cracked walls and use the way they chain. The exact layout of rooms and chests may differ in the remake (not yet confirmed), but this is the structure:

- **Entrance and great hall:** Baby Dodongos, Dodongos and Beamos. Dodongos go down best to bombs, and you knock out Beamos by throwing one at them. Look for the **Map** in the rooms on the east side of the ground floor.
- **East wing:** chain Bomb Flowers to open the way and light the unlit torches. Two fights against Lizalfos that switch sides wait for you; guard and counter. The **Compass** is in one of the side rooms.
- **Bomb Flower staircase:** in the staircase room, set off the Bomb Flowers so the stairs drop, and climb to the upper floor.
- **Bomb Bag:** in a chest on the upper floor, after the last Lizalfos fight (exact room not yet confirmed for the remake). From now on you take out bombs with ZR.
- **The giant skull:** cross the bridge over the great hall and drop a bomb into each eye of the huge Dodongo skull; they light up and the mouth opens. Inside, push the block into the hole and drop down to the boss room.

**Gold Skulltulas #30–34:** #30 is in the Bomb Flower room (knock it down with the Slingshot), #31 and #32 are behind bombable walls in the east corridor and in the north, #33 is above the staircase (with the Boomerang) and #34 needs the Scarecrow’s Song as an adult.

## Boss: King Dodongo

King Dodongo moves around a ring surrounding a lava pit. When he opens his mouth to inhale before breathing fire:

1. Take out a bomb (ZR) and throw it straight into his mouth. A Bomb Flower from the corners works too.
2. He swallows it, it explodes and he collapses. Dash in holding A and slash his head (Y).
3. When he gets up he curls into a ball and rolls around the ring: step aside and jump (X) if you need to.

Three or four cycles do it. Do not stand between him and the lava, and keep clear of his fire. Once he falls, press A to pick up **Heart Container #2**.

Outside, Darunia names you his sworn brother and gives you the **Goron Ruby**. Climb to the summit: blow up the wall next to the owl with a bomb and enter the Great Fairy Fountain, which gives you the **Magic Meter** and, according to Zelda Wiki, in the remake Din’s Fire as well (not yet confirmed). Afterwards, the owl flies you back to the village.

> **Don’t leave without:** Saria’s Song, the Sun’s Song, the Bomb Bag, the Magic Meter and what you can now solve with bombs: Gold Skulltulas #99 and #100 in Hyrule Field and Piece of Heart #5 at Bombchu Bowling.
