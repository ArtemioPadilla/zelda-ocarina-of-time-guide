With the Kokiri Emerald in your pocket, you leave the forest for the first time. Kaepora Gaebora, the owl, waits at the edge of Hyrule Field and points you to the castle. This chapter is about exploring: the castle, the ranch and the field’s first secrets.

> **Order:** Hyrule Field → Hyrule Castle Town → sneaking into the castle → Zelda and Zelda’s Lullaby → Lon Lon Ranch and Epona’s Song → on to Kakariko Village.

## Hyrule Field

The remake’s field is no longer a big empty plain: it has waterfalls, ruins and groves, and more enemies come out by day (Tektites, Peahat Larvae). Stalchildren appear at night; if they bother you, sleep in a bedroll or a bed to change the time. Lake Hylia is part of the same field map, with no loading screen.

Grottos are no longer invisible: look for cracked ground or marked rocks. Many open with bombs, so come back after Dodongo’s Cavern. The field holds three Skulltulas in the remake (there used to be two): **Gold Skulltula #99**, under the ring of stones past the turn-off to Gerudo Valley, and **#100**, in the lone tree east of the Castle Town entrance; both need bombs. The third one has no confirmed location yet.

## Hyrule Castle Town

The whole town, Temple of Time included, is now **a single map with a free camera**, with more people and their own schedules: many townsfolk go to bed at night and their lines change with the time of day.

- **Bazaar:** sells the Hylian Shield. It is still too big for you, but you will use it as an adult and it stands up to fire.
- **Potion Shop:** help the owner with his errand and he gives you the **fifth bottle**, new in the remake. Do it early: bottles hold fairies, bugs and potions.
- **Piece of Heart #3:** at night, a white dog roams the back streets. Get it to follow you to its owner’s house.
- **Piece of Heart #4** (Treasure Chest game) and **#5** (Bombchu Bowling): the first is easier with the Lenses of Truth and the second needs the Bomb Bag; leave them for later.
- **Gold Skulltula #8:** inside a crate in the guardhouse by the drawbridge (3DS position, not yet confirmed for the remake). Roll into the crate to break it.

## Sneaking into the castle

In the remake the route to the courtyard is **new**: you get in through a small sewer, cross a rooftop garden and slip through an interior storage room. Jumping with X and the free camera make it easier than before: watch each guard’s round before you move and go when they turn their back. If they spot you, they send you back to the entrance; you lose nothing but time.

On the way to the castle are **Gold Skulltula #9** (in the tree ringed by rocks: roll into the trunk) and **#10**, which needs the Song of Storms and bombs, so it is for much later.

In the courtyard you find Zelda peering through the window (in the remake you will see the Threads of Time tapestry there). She tells you her dream about Ganondorf and hands you **Zelda’s Letter**. On the way out, Impa teaches you **Zelda’s Lullaby** (Y X A Y X A), the song that opens Triforce symbols all through the game. You can check your songs at any time by holding ZL when you take out the Ocarina.

With Zelda’s Letter, the guard at the Kakariko Village gate lets you up Death Mountain, and the Castle Town mask shop starts lending you masks.

## Lon Lon Ranch

South of the field is Lon Lon Ranch, redesigned with a look closer to Breath of the Wild. Talk to Malon in the corral once you have the Ocarina: she teaches you **Epona’s Song** (X Y A X Y A). Play it near the foal to make friends; you will need her as an adult.

- **Piece of Heart #6:** in the big barn, push a crate and crawl through the gap it leaves.
- **Gold Skulltulas #11–14:** they all come out at night around the ranch; #13 and #14 need the Boomerang. In the remake the ranch has **three** Skulltulas instead of four, so one of these moved or is gone (not yet confirmed which).
- Talon’s Cucco minigame gives a bottle of milk (not yet confirmed whether the remake changed it).

## The field’s two new Pieces of Heart

The remake changed two Hyrule Field pieces:

- **Piece of Heart #19:** a Business Scrub in a grotto near Hyrule Castle, north-east of Castle Town, sells it for **100 Rupees**, and only if you tell him a password. Navi reads you that password from a Fairy Thought by the Castle Town entrance, but only as an adult, and **as an adult the Business Scrub has sold out**: find him now, read the password once you are an adult and come back as a child to buy it.
- **Piece of Heart #20:** it is now a chest on a rock outcrop solved with time travel. As a child it is sealed and as an adult it is empty: read the Fairy Thought there as an adult and come back as a child to open it. Note it for after the Master Sword.

## The Great Fairy by the castle

On the way to the castle there is a Great Fairy Fountain blocked by a rock you blow up with bombs. The remake’s Great Fairies ask for a Rupee offering that grows with each visit. Zelda Wiki says this fairy now gives the **improved Spin Attack** and that Din’s Fire moved to the Death Mountain fountain; other guides still give the original split (not yet confirmed for the remake).

> **Don’t leave without:** Zelda’s Lullaby, Epona’s Song, Zelda’s Letter and the fifth bottle. Also note the Business Scrub grotto for Piece of Heart #19: you will have to come back as a child with 100 Rupees and the password.
