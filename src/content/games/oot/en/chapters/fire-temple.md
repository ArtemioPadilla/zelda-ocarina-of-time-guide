Ganondorf has revived Volvagia and dragged the Gorons into the Fire Temple to feed them to it. Climb Death Mountain as an adult. In the remake the heat is more dangerous than ever: without the Goron Tunic, hot areas drain your health at once and **lava kills on contact**.

> **Order:** empty Goron City → stop Link the Goron (Goron Tunic) → Darunia’s room → Death Mountain Crater → Bolero of Fire → Fire Temple → the Goron cells → Megaton Hammer → Volvagia → Fire Medallion.

## Goron City and the Goron Tunic

The city is almost empty. On the middle floor a Goron keeps rolling around: it is Darunia’s son, named **Link** after you. Throw a bomb (or a Bomb Flower) in his path to stop him. He tells you what happened, gives you the **Goron Tunic** and opens his father’s room. In the remake there is no heat timer anymore: with the tunic on you can stay in the crater and the temple as long as you like, and without it you lose health very quickly.

**Gold Skulltula #36** is on the hanging platform in the middle of the city, within Hookshot reach. In Darunia’s room, push the statue at the back to open the way to the crater.

## Death Mountain Crater

Cross the bridge. Sheik appears and teaches you the **Bolero of Fire** (R L R L A R A R), which warps you to the crater. Use the Hookshot to reach the far side of the broken bridge and climb down the ladder towards the temple.

- **Gold Skulltula #37:** in a crate just inside the crater entrance. **#38:** in the soft soil next to the Bolero warp point, with bugs.
- **Piece of Heart #31:** enter the crater from above, from the top of the trail, and climb down the wall to the ledge.
- **Piece of Heart #32:** at the top of the smoking column; ride the Magic Bean plant you sowed as a child.
- **Piece of Heart #29:** in the remake it is no longer in the Ice Cavern: a crater Business Scrub sells it **to child Link** for 100 Rupees. Reach it through Darunia’s room in your child era. Nintendo Life also mentions a Deku Seed trick for getting to him.
- **Crater Great Fairy:** behind a rock you smash with the Hammer, she upgrades your Magic Meter. She asks for a Rupee offering that grows with each fairy.

## Fire Temple

Darunia goes in ahead of you and asks you to free the imprisoned Gorons. Each Goron is in a cell with its own switch, and almost every cell holds a small key. Exact room and chest positions are from the base game (not yet confirmed for the remake); what is known is that heat and lava punish you harder.

- **First room:** the boss door is on the left, over the lava, but you cannot reach it yet. Go right and free the first Goron.
- **Lava room:** bridges, fire walls and fences to climb. Go up the fences, make your jumps with X and do not stop on the edges: falling into lava is certain death in the remake.
- **Flare Dancer:** the mini-boss appears wrapped in flames. Hookshot it out of the fire and slash it while it runs around without its cloak of flames. You meet it twice.
- **Rolling boulder maze and fire walls:** the **Map** and **Compass** are here (room not yet confirmed). Use the Compass to see which cells you still need.
- **Megaton Hammer:** at the top of the temple, after the second Flare Dancer fight. It smashes rocks and pounds down rusted blocks and heavy switches. Set it on ZR.
- **Going down with the Hammer:** smash the blocks that sealed stairs and cells on the way down, free the remaining Gorons and take the **Boss Key** from one of those cells. Pound the marked floor on the top level with the Hammer: the great pillar crashes into the lava by the entrance and opens the way to the boss door.

**Gold Skulltulas #62–66:** #62 in the room on the east side of the ground floor (play the Song of Time to move the block), #63 behind the cracked wall of the rolling boulder room (with a bomb), #64 on the fence you reach on the rising platform (with the Scarecrow’s Song), #65 on the wall of the room with the big hole on the fifth floor (with the Hookshot) and #66 behind the door next to the entrance stairs. You teach the Scarecrow’s Song to Bonooru in Lake Hylia as a child and repeat it to Pierre as an adult.

## Boss: Volvagia

The arena is a platform with nine holes in the lava. Keep the Goron Tunic on.

1. Volvagia pokes her head out of a hole. Get close and hit it with the **Megaton Hammer** (ZR) before she ducks back in.
2. While she is stunned, switch to the sword and land as many blows on the head as you can.
3. When she flies, she sweeps fire across the arena and brings rocks down from the ceiling: follow the shadows on the floor and dash (hold A) out from under them.

Early reviews mention slightly tweaked patterns for some bosses without saying which (not yet confirmed for Volvagia). Once she falls, press A to pick up **Heart Container #5**. Darunia awakens as the Sage of Fire and gives you the **Fire Medallion**.

## After the temple

With the Hammer you can now smash the rocks on the mountain trail and collect **Gold Skulltulas #28 and #29**. Biggoron sits at the summit, the end of the trading sequence that ends in the **Biggoron Sword**, the strongest in the game. See the dedicated side quest for the steps.

> **Don’t leave without:** the Goron Tunic on before you head down into the crater, the Megaton Hammer, Piece of Heart #29 bought as a child from the crater Business Scrub, and Gold Skulltulas #28 and #29.
