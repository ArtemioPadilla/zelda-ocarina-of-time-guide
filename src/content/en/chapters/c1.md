## Walkthrough

You wake up with Navi, who leads you to speak with the Great Deku Tree — he's just asked you to clear the curse that's killing him. Before heading in, explore Kokiri Forest: Mido blocks the path until you show him you have a fairy; the Kokiri Shop sells the Deku Shield and Deku Nuts; and the hidden grove behind the hollow log holds a chest with the **Kokiri Sword**, essential before entering the dungeon.

Inside the Deku Tree, use your new sword and shield to fight through Deku Babas and Gohma Larvae. The **Fairy Slingshot** turns up in a chest partway through — light distant torches with it and knock down webs to reveal chests and Skulltulas. The circular vine room lifts you several floors; follow the web platforms up to the boss room.

> 💡 Shoot the Deku Babas' red eyes with the Slingshot before closing in — it saves you several hits.

## Boss — Queen Gohma

Wait for the eye on her abdomen to open and hit it with the Slingshot to stun her; rush in and slash while she's down. Watch for the Gohma Larvae dropping from the ceiling — a Deku Nut clears them all at once.

Defeating her gets you the **Kokiri's Emerald Spiritual Stone**, and the dying Deku Tree tells you the truth about Ganondorf and your origin before he passes.
