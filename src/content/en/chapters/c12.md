## Walkthrough

The castle crumbles around you as you scramble through the rubble to the outside, where Ganondorf resurfaces in his beast form: **Ganon**. Zelda uses her magic to hold him in place while the six Sages lend you their strength; the final fight takes place in the smoking ruins right outside the castle.

Ganon attacks with charges and shockwaves whenever he slams the ground. The **Sun's Song** briefly stuns him the instant the fight starts — use the opening to slice off his tail with your sword, which knocks him down. With the tail gone, attack his head while he's on the ground; repeat the cycle until Zelda can hold him fully in place for the finishing blow with the **Master Sword**.

> 💡 If you run low on Light Arrows or potions mid-fight, the grass patches around the arena still occasionally drop something useful.

## Ending

With Ganon sealed away in the Sacred Realm, Zelda uses the Ocarina of Time to send Link back to his childhood — undoing the seven lost years, but also giving him the chance to live them alongside his friends in Kokiri Forest. The guide ends here: Ocarina of Time's real postgame is its sidequests, so check the **Sidequests** page if you've still got loose ends to tie up.
